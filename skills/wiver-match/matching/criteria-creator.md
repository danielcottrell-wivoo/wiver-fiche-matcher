## Read the fiche and set the grille

Start from the fiche alone, before reading any ledger, so the grille reflects the job rather than the pool. Fiches are dense and written in the client's vocabulary, and their title does not always match the work. Work out the core work of the job: what the consultant will actually do day to day. Tools, sector and education listed in the fiche are secondary.

Every fiche implies a grille: the few things that decide who can do this job. Make it explicit with 3 to 6 criteria, or more if you judge it necessary. Phrase each one as work to have done, not as keywords or tools. Mark three criteria as core, with ★: they decide the verdict, while the others only order people within a verdict. Core criteria describe the domain: the kind of job, and the kind of product and people it serves. Specifics such as a technology, a sector or a tool only order people within a verdict, unless the fiche makes them a hard requirement. Clients are often flexible on specifics, and the sales person knows how much. Sector, or prior work at this client, becomes a criterion only when the fiche makes it matter. Seniority is always assessed, separately.

The reply is built on the ★ compétences, in this order, each with a short label of 2 to 3 words (« ★ Business analysis », « ★ IA / data »).

Then look for the points flous: passages of the fiche that are ambiguous, or major information it leaves out (the weight of a secondary scope, whether the client wants a pure Business Analyst or would take a Product Owner…), when the answer would change who fits. Keep only those, from 0 to 3. For each, decide the hypothesis you will take if the sales person does not know.

Show the user, before reading any ledger:

- The core criteria as a two-column table, « Critère » and « Ce que la fiche attend »: one row per ★ criterion, with its short label and the work to have done, then rows for Connaissance secteur, Séniorité and Disponibilité. When the fiche is unclear on one of them, the second cell says how you read it (for example « Séniorité » and « pas de durée dans la fiche ; « forte expérience confirmée » lu comme 5 ans et plus »).
- Then the other criteria of the grille in one line, introduced with « Autres critères pris en compte, hors cœur : ».
- If the fiche gives no start date, ask how far ahead to look: « Il n'y a pas de date de démarrage dans la fiche : je regarde les consultants disponibles maintenant ou d'ici la fin du mois ? Ou plus loin ? » Without an answer, look up to the end of the month. Consultants available after that horizon stay out of the Top.
- If there are any, the points flous under « **Points flous** », one bullet each: the passage or the missing information, the hypothesis you will take by default, and what would change if it were wrong.

Introduce it with « Voilà les points centraux que j'ai retenus pour évaluer le match entre les consultants et la fiche. Avant de lancer l'analyse, tu es d'accord ? Est-ce que tu as des choses à ajouter ? ». When there are points flous, end with « Si tu as plus d'infos sur ces points ou sur le contexte du client, dis-le moi : ça m'aidera à mieux matcher. » Then wait for the answer.

What the sales person says about a point flou replaces its hypothesis; a point left unanswered keeps it. The reply does not come back to the points flous: they are settled here.

If the fiche describes more than one role (for example a project manager plus technical profiles), ask in the same message which one to match, or both. For several roles, produce one grille and one tab per role.
