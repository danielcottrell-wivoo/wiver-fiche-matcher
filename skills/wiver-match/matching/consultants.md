## Consultants and ledgers

Everything this step needs is in Notion, so use the Notion connector only, not Drive. Ledgers are maintained separately; take them as they are.

The Notion page "Ledgers et Consultants en inter-mission" (`3ea331cabede8076b82ae0acc07cb47a`) lists the consultants looking for a mission. Follow its instructions to get each one's name, availability date and statut de mission.

Its child page "Ledgers" (`3eb331cabede8000a6fcd7e0da4f3274`) holds one page per consultant, titled `ledger_prenom_nom` in lowercase without accents: a complete record of what their DCs say, written for you to match against. Match each consultant to their ledger by name, loosely, since names are written a little differently across sources (accents, particles, hyphens). Read the ledger of every consultant on the list.
